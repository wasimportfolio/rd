FAQ

Questions, answered

Most of our couples lock their date 6-9 months out, especially for peak wedding season weekends in Tamil Nadu. A small advance secures your date on our calendar — the balance is due closer to the event.

A curated preview gallery goes out within 72 hours. Full edited photos are delivered in 2-3 weeks, and your cinematic wedding film follows in 3-4 weeks depending on the season.

Yes — we regularly travel across South India and beyond for destination weddings. Travel, stay and logistics are quoted separately based on the location and duration.

We deliver our fully edited, color-graded selection rather than raw files — this is where our style and craft actually happen. Raw footage add-ons can be discussed for videography packages on request.

Absolutely — album design and printing is one of our core services. We'll shortlist your favourite frames together and hand-craft a layout before sending it for premium printing.
